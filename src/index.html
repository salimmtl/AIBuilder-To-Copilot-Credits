AI Builder Credit Analyzer

Reads AI Builder activity from the connected Dataverse environment and estimates the equivalent consumption in Copilot Credits.

Scan every environment. Power Platform ToolBox connects to one environment at a time, so this reads only the environment currently connected. To see your whole tenant, switch the connection and scan again — results accumulate below until you clear them.
Estimate, not a bill. Each run is matched to an AI Builder capability from its model template, language model, tool name or data type, and converted at that capability's published rate. That matching is a best effort and will not be right every time — prompt tiers in particular are inferred from the language model and can be wrong. Treat the result as a directional estimate of equivalent Copilot Credit consumption, not a guaranteed figure. Every run carries a confidence rating, and anything that cannot be identified is reported rather than silently costed.
